# Research guardrails

1. No copyrighted book text, EPUBs, ordered token sequences, or secrets in git.
2. Preserve historical results and tolerances. Corrections require a new version.
3. No fabricated data or claims of execution without source bytes and checks.
4. Require independent reproduction, controls, and uncertainty before promoting findings.
5. Treat POV, mentions, coappearance, interaction, belief and causal influence as distinct.
