What this does not measure.

A silence is the number of included chapters between two recorded chapters owned by the same POV character. It does not mean the character is absent. A one-chapter viewpoint has no observed return, not a zero gap. EPUB chapter-token totals differ from the curated POV-source word totals. These three book orders are not an independently validated in-world event chronology. The 42 equal-token windows control sample size, not narrative position, author, or scene segmentation.
