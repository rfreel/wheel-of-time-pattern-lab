07 / How to read the instrument

Evidence is not prophecy.

Every displayed quantity is descriptive. Literary interpretation starts where the numeric evidence ends.

What is measured

Length: word-like tokens in EPUB narrative chapters, with editorial front/back matter excluded.

Seams: Jensen–Shannon divergence in bits between adjacent chapter token-frequency distributions, no smoothing, base-2 logarithms.

Echoes: exact whole-paragraph equality after whitespace normalization; not paraphrases or substring matches.

What is not established

Co-occurrence is not friendship. Lexical novelty is not worldbuilding quality. A verbatim return is not independently proven foreshadowing. The random-order test does not control for chapter length or character names.

Data derived from the user-provided EPUB corpus and versioned WOT Article Core v1 and Continuity v1 numerical exports. Original packages remain unchanged. No prose, EPUB text or external assets embedded.
