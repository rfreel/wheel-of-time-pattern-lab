FIELD NOTE / 001

For hundreds of chapters, a character can be present but never own the point of view. In the finale, several familiar people finally speak from within. At the same time, the final three books change narrators across chapter boundaries far more frequently. These drawings show the measured distances, not an invented explanation of why they exist.
